How to read these numbers
  • All figures are from Ramp Data: card and bill-pay spending by US businesses on Ramp. Free tiers and tools paid for another way are not counted.
  • "AI tool" adoption (tiles, sectors, sizes) comes from Ramp's AI Index. Category figures come from Ramp Rate, which measures each category separately, so the two are not directly comparable.
  • Vendor shares overlap: one business can pay several vendors in the same category.
  • Which categories count as "AI tools" is this dashboard's own grouping.
